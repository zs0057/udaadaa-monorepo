## 🏃‍♀️ udaadaa

udaadaa is a diet management application that helps people stay committed to their health goals through a chatting room

## ❓ Why We Built This

Most of my friends failed at dieting, and I concluded that weak motivation was the cause of their failure. Therefore, I created udaadaa to solve this problem by fostering an environment where people can  together.

### **[Key Decision: Resource Optimization]**
Our team's mission was to create a service that *actually* helps people's diets. To stay true to this mission, we needed to move fast. By choosing **Flutter** and **Supabase**, we reduced repetitive tasks and focused our limited resources on enhancing the user-centric challenge experience.

## ⭐️ result

- User Feedback: Successfully launched and received positive feedback from 20+ initial users.

- Retention: Maintained a 44% user retention rate through continuous, feedback-driven updates.

- Growth: Beyond coding, I learned the invaluable lesson of building software that truly connects with and serves people.
